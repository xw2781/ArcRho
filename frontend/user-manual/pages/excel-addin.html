Formula rules

Useful formula behavior

Triangle options

  • Cumulative TRUE returns cumulative triangle.
  • Transposed TRUE flips display orientation.
  • Calendar TRUE requests calendar view.
  • Origin and Development Length choose period aggregation.
  • Manual and generated datasets support compatible coarser periods from their published values, such as monthly exposure rolled up to annual exposure.
  • A generated dataset asked for at a finer period or another mode than its publication, such as quarterly exposure when the frontend shows it annually, is calculated by the Arco Engine from the source table. The published values are never changed.
  • Manual, calculated, and method outputs can only be read at their published shape or a compatible coarser one.

Saved workbook results

  • Opening and ordinary recalculation read the last saved Arco snapshot without contacting the server.
  • Typing, editing, or pasting a formula loads missing results immediately after entry, including when you change its explicit project name. A request already saved in the workbook reuses those values.
  • Explicit refresh fetches each distinct request once. A failed or cancelled refresh preserves the previous snapshot.
  • Save after adding formulas or refreshing to share the results. Older workbooks need one refresh and save for their existing formulas.
  • Permanent datasets are read as published. Temporary generated results are reused while their source and processing settings remain unchanged.

Pickers

  • Load Reserving Classes writes path parts into active cells.
  • Select Datasets filters by category and format.
  • Settings stores paths used by the add-in.
