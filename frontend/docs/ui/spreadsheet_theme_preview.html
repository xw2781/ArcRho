What Changes From Current

  • Grid lines move from a warm near-white to a cool slate that holds on white, and a slightly darker seam marks where the header and row labels meet the values.
  • Header and labels drop the heavy gray fill for a pale tint, so the grid lines, not the fills, carry the structure.
  • Selection keeps its four meanings (general, percentage, input, result) in softer fills, with a one-pixel range edge and an accent line on the matching row and column labels.
  • Tabs become flat text with one accent underline instead of boxed folder tabs.
  • Links keep their green, blue and purple outlines unchanged.
